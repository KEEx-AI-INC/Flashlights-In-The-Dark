# Fall 2026 24-system / 12-page compact cast

## Fixed requirement

Use exactly **24 musical systems on 12 nonblank Letter-size music pages**, with
exactly two systems on every page. Page 1 remains fixed at m.1-3 / m.4-6. The
edition has no blank or separate colophon page in this profile, so the complete
score body is already divisible by four for booklet production.

This plan supersedes the earlier 39-music-page publication cast. It is a
casting-only source plan: it does not authorize any musical, lyric, direction,
staff-line, show-control, or runtime change. It also does not claim collision
clearance before a complete Dorico render is inspected.

Begin the proof at **1.20 mm staff size, 8.5 pt lyrics, and 8 pt directions**.
The hard readability floor is **1.10 mm staff size, 8 pt lyrics, and 7.5 pt
directions**. The first proof is 80% of the earlier 1.50 mm staff evidence
baseline, with a comparable reduction from the 10.5 pt lyric baseline. Keep
chord symbols at 8 pt or larger. These are Dorico proof settings, not MusicXML
mutations. If the 1.20 mm proof collides, adjust global and local spacing before
reducing size; do not cross the hard floor merely to preserve the map.

Use full staff names only on the first system and compact, unambiguous
abbreviations thereafter. Retain the Shadow/Light brackets and group barlines.
Do not save horizontal space by hiding staves, changing five-line staves, or
switching lyric lanes syllable by syllable. Apply fixed two-systems-per-frame
casting only after the music-frame margins and page-1 furniture band are
stable.

Render and inspect every page after the compact scale and vertical spacing are
stable. Reject the proof if any page has a system-on-system overlap, staff or
frame clipping, unstable lyric lanes, obscured dynamics or chord symbols,
fragmented instructions, or unreadable text. Give 100% inspection priority to
m.1-22, m.37-70, m.81-103, m.104-129, and m.140-151, including the chord block
near m.93-98 and the aleatoric instruction that must begin the p.11 system at
m.115.

The visual acceptance gate is all-or-nothing:

1. Confirm exactly 12 Letter pages, exactly 24 systems, exactly two systems on
   every page, all six five-line staves on every system, and no blank page or
   automatic extra system.
2. Render every page at 300 dpi and inspect full pages plus all dense systems at
   100%. Reject any literal overlap, clipping, fused chord/text object, or
   unstable lyric lane.
3. Inspect the facing boundaries m.13/14, m.36/37, m.57/58, m.92/93, and
   m.114/115 as spreads. These may carry active material, but they must remain
   simultaneously visible without a physical page turn.
4. Print-test pages 1-3, 5, 7, 9, and 12 at actual Letter size. The hard floor
   is not accepted merely because the PDF is collision-free; singers must be
   able to read the lyrics, voice lanes, dynamics, and chord/instruction cues
   from a choir folder.
5. Re-export MusicXML and run the complete semantic validation before final PDF
   assembly. The 12-page music score is already booklet-compatible; do not
   append the old page-40 colophon as a thirteenth page.
