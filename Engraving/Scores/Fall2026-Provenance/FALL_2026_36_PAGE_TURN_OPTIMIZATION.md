## Recommendation

Use **35 music pages plus the nonblank page-36 colophon**, with the revised
36-system segmentation below. Page 1 remains the only two-system music page.
Every later music page carries exactly one system.

The Stage M measure ranges cannot simply be reassigned to different pages. With
36 systems on 35 music pages, and page 1 already required to contain exactly two
systems, page 1 consumes the edition's only possible paired page. Pages 2-35
must therefore each contain exactly one system. If all Stage M system boundaries
remain fixed, its page numbering and all of its right-hand turns are
mathematically fixed as well.

The smallest useful remedy is a temporary one-page parity shift:

1. Recast the three Stage M systems 21-22, 23-25, and 26-31 as two
   systems, 21-24 and 25-31. This shifts all following systems one page
   earlier and lands the stronger m.31 turn on page 7.
2. Move one bar between the chord/lyric systems, changing 93-97 and
   98-101 to 93-96 and 97-101. This moves the right-hand turn away from
   "should your / hands" and retains the complete "car-ry ... yours too"
   passage through the next spread.
3. Split the Stage M system 119-124 into 119-121 and 122-124. This
   restores the original page parity after all four critical turn problems have
   passed, while keeping the final m.125-151 systems unchanged.

This plan eliminates the four specified hard turns without putting any of the
previously colliding two-system pairs back on one page. It also improves the
turn after page 7 and several later turns. The remaining unsafe turns are at
musically documented problem points rather than inside a word.

## Acceptance gate

This is a casting plan, not a visual acceptance claim. After implementing the
break delta, export a new proof and inspect all 35 music pages. The priority
checks are pages 6-7, 21-23, and 29-30 at 100 percent, followed by every
right-hand spread. Reject the 35-page map if either m.21-24, m.25-31, or
m.97-101 becomes horizontally unreadable after the final 10.5 pt lyric and
9.5-10 pt direction settings.

A 39-music-page plus colophon page-40 edition is not yet warranted. It would
require increasing the score from 36 to 40 systems, producing four additional
one-system pages and new parity decisions. The bounded 36-system revision above
solves the four critical turns with fewer structural changes and should be
proved visually first. Escalate to 40 total pages only if one of the three
changed dense systems fails the next full-page render and cannot be corrected
without shrinking the approved typography.
