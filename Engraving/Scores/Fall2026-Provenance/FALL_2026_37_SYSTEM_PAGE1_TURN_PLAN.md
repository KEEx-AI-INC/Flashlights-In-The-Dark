## Recommendation

Use **37 musical systems on 35 music pages**, followed by the nonblank page-36
colophon.

- Page 1 contains exactly two short systems: m.1-3 and m.4-6.
- Page 2 contains the former second title-page system, m.7-10.
- Page 29 is the only additional two-system page: m.113-114 and m.115-118.
- Every other music page contains one system.

This is the best turn-aware map for the required page-1 repair. The extra
opening system shifts the Stage M pagination by one page, putting all four
previously unacceptable boundaries on visible even-to-odd spreads. Pairing the
short m.113-114 continuation with m.115-118 on page 29 recovers the required
35-music-page total only after the last of those four boundaries has passed.
The pair also preserves the strong later turns after m.129 and at the sectional
m.139/m.140 boundary.

The page-29 pairing is the best available post-blocker candidate, but it remains
a visual acceptance item. Stage M proves each component system clean and shows
ample unused lower-page space; it does not prove the two together under the
current house style. The paired proof must therefore be rendered before final
acceptance.
