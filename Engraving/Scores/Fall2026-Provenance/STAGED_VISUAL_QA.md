# Stage D visual QA

Audited source: `FlashlightsInTheDark_Fall2026_PerformerScore_StageD.pdf`

Comparison source: `FlashlightsInTheDark_Fall2026_PerformerScore_StageC.pdf`

Audit date: 2026-08-22

Method: all 15 Stage D pages were rendered at 240 dpi (2040 x 2640 pixels per Letter page), inspected as complete pages, and re-inspected at every system boundary and in the named high-risk passages.

## Verdict

**Fail - not publishable or rehearsal-ready.** Stage D successfully places exactly two systems on page 1, but it reduces the proof to 15 pages and repacks all 30 systems two-per-page. This shifts the Stage C overlap failures to new pages rather than resolving them. Nine pages now contain severe or catastrophic system-boundary collisions, and the chord-symbol, lyric-routing, grouping, title-credit, and measure-115 problems remain.

## Stage C to Stage D comparison

### Improved

- Page 1 now contains exactly two systems (m.1-5 and m.6-9).
- The `musique concrète` passage is now isolated cleanly on page 10 instead of being caught in a system overlap.
- Page 14's Stage C mangled running title (`FlaDark`) is restored to the full title.

### Regressed or unresolved

- Page count changed from 16 to 15. Fifteen is not divisible by four and is not saddle-stitch-ready.
- Stage C's severe collisions were not fixed; the new pairing of systems creates catastrophic overlaps on pages 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, and 15.
- Page 1's title/credit/instruction collisions remain, and `Set in 2076` plus the commission line are still absent.
- Chord symbols at m.93-97 remain unacceptably congested.
- The m.115 instruction remains duplicated and colliding; related repetitions continue at m.126 onward.
- Long imported lyric-extension lines continue to strike through words and secondary lyric lanes.
- One bracket still spans all six staves; the required Shadow/Light grouping and group barlines are absent.

## Lyric-routing audit

The corrected text is present, but engraving/routing is not stable:

- long extenders strike through words on pages 7, 11, and 12;

- repeated or duplicate text remains in the same visual lane (`What are wonders`, `listen for primer tone`, and the aleatoric instruction);
- several isolated parentheses/extenders remain visible without a clear lyric context.

## Required before another publishability review

1. Do not retain the all-pages-two-systems constraint. Keep page 1 at exactly two systems, but allow dense later pages to carry one system.
2. Recast to a page count divisible by four; given the content, 20 pages is a more plausible next proof than compressing to 16 or 15.
3. Stabilize lyric lanes and remove imported extension lines before local collision cleanup.
4. Convert ensemble-wide instructions to one semantic system object and eliminate duplicated staff copies.
5. Restore the two Shadow/Light brackets and group barlines.
6. Rebuild page 1's title/credit frame, including `Set in 2076` and the commission line.
7. Re-space m.93-97 chord symbols and re-engrave m.115 before the next full-page export.
