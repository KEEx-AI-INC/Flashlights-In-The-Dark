# Stage E visual QA

Audited source: `FlashlightsInTheDark_Fall2026_PerformerScore_StageE.pdf`

Comparison source: `FlashlightsInTheDark_Fall2026_PerformerScore_StageD.pdf`

Audit date: 2026-08-22

Method: all 15 Stage E Letter pages were rendered at 240 dpi (2040 x 2640 pixels), inspected as complete pages, and re-inspected at original render size at every system boundary and in the named high-risk passages. PDF dimensions, page count, and embedded font objects were checked separately.

## Verdict

**Fail - materially improved, but not publishable or rehearsal-ready.** Stage E makes two important structural advances: it restores two separate three-staff brackets and reduces severe/catastrophic system-on-system collisions from nine Stage D pages to five Stage E pages. It also eliminates every one of Stage D's nine former collision boundaries. However, five newly paired boundaries now collide, page 1 remains severely congested, lyrics and imported extension lines remain unreadable in many systems, m.93-97 chord symbols remain crowded, and the aleatoric directions are still duplicated. The file is 15 pages, so it is not booklet-compatible as delivered.

The proposed 24-page cast should remove all five remaining **vertical system-boundary collisions**, because each affected boundary will either be absorbed into one system or separated onto another page. It will not by itself repair horizontal lyric fusion, extension lines through words, chord-symbol spacing, duplicated directions, group barlines/names, or page-1 furniture.

## Focused high-risk audit

### Page 1 / m.1-10

- Exactly two systems: **pass**.
- Two group brackets: **pass**.
- Title and credit frame: **fail**.
- Text and lyric readability: **fail**.
- Missing `Set in 2076` and commission line: **fail**.

### M.93-97

- Vertical separation from the previous system: **pass**.
- Chord-symbol readability: **fail**. A 24-page document that retains m.93-97 as one system will not change this horizontal spacing. Local chord-symbol work or a narrower measure allocation is still required.

### M.115 and the anticipated m.126 instruction

- M.115 has sufficient top-of-page room, but its instruction is duplicated twice and must become one system-wide semantic object.
- No aleatoric instruction is visible at m.126 in Stage E. The second instance begins at m.130 and is duplicated three times. This should be reconciled against editorial intent before final cleanup.
- The 24-page cast's one-system m.115 page will remove the m.121/122 boundary collision and give the direction ample room, but it will not deduplicate it automatically.

## Typography and lyric routing

The base typefaces are suitable and nominal text sizes are readable when objects are unobstructed. Running titles, page numbers, the m.115 direction, and the sparse m.80-88 page show that the Academico/Bravura house style can work at this staff size.

The present proof nevertheless fails the practical readability test because placement overwhelms nominal size:

- phrases fuse horizontally on pages 1-7;
- upper/lower voice text still shares or switches lanes on pages 1, 5-7, 11, and 12;
- extension lines run through words on pages 5-7 and 11-12;
- dynamics and lyrics collide on pages 3, 5-7, 12, and 15;
- the aleatoric instruction is semantically duplicated rather than hierarchically system-wide.

This is not primarily a request for a smaller font. The systems need more horizontal room, stable voice-specific lyric lanes, and local cleanup after the new cast is fixed.

## Effect of the proposed 24-page cast

The forthcoming cast is a valid structural response to every remaining Stage E system-boundary collision:

| Stage E collision | 24-page treatment | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| m.34/35 | Both fall inside the one-system m.32-36 page | Boundary collision removed |
| m.64/65 | Both fall inside the one-system m.62-66 page | Boundary collision removed |
| m.75/76 | Both fall inside the one-system m.71-76 page | Boundary collision removed |
| m.121/122 | Both fall inside the m.119-124 system | Boundary collision removed |
| m.132/133 | Both fall inside the m.130-134 system | Boundary collision removed |

Therefore the 24-page cast should eliminate the five literal vertical cross-system conflicts if Dorico honors the intended breaks. It also gives m.115 a one-system page and produces a booklet-compatible total when page 24 is a real nonblank colophon.

It is **not sufficient as a final engraving pass**. After importing/casting, the next proof still requires:

1. rebuild page 1's title, credit, instruction, and copyright frames;
2. consolidate the m.115 and m.130 directions as system-wide objects and verify the intended second measure location;
3. re-space or subdivide m.93-97 for chord-symbol clarity;
4. route upper/lower/third lyric voices into stable lanes and remove legacy extension lines;
5. join barlines within each ensemble and replace numeric group labels with `Shadow Chorus` and `Light Chorus`;
6. perform local dynamic, slur, lyric, and direction collision cleanup page by page.

Stage E is a useful structural intermediate, not a publishable performer score.
