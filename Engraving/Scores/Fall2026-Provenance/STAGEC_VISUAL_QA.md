# Stage C visual QA

Audited source: `FlashlightsInTheDark_Fall2026_PerformerScore_StageC.pdf`

Audit date: 2026-08-22

Method: all 16 pages rendered at 240 dpi (2040 x 2640 pixels per Letter page), inspected as complete pages, and re-inspected in dense/system-boundary zones. Page ranges were read from the printed measure numbers and rehearsal marks.

## Verdict

**Fail - not publishable or rehearsal-ready.** The export is musically populated and passes several basic preflight checks, but the current casting creates literal system-on-system collisions on multiple pages. Lyrics, instructions, staff labels, notes, and rehearsal furniture are overwritten in the densest passages. Page 1 also violates the explicit two-system requirement.

## Basic preflight

- Pass: 16 pages, all US Letter portrait (612 x 792 pt).
- Pass: no blank pages.
- Pass: every visible staff is five-line.
- Pass: Academico, Academico Bold/Italic, Bravura, and Bravura Text are embedded as subset fonts.
- Pass: outside page-number placement is generally correct (even pages left, odd pages right).
- Fail: there is one bracket spanning all six staves, not two bracketed Shadow/Light ensembles; group barlines are not restored.
- Fail: page 1 has one system, not the required two.
- Fail: later-page running titles are inconsistent or damaged (missing on several even pages; mangled on page 14).
- Fail: none of the right-hand page turns after pages 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, and 15 lands at a usable rest or sectional pause.
