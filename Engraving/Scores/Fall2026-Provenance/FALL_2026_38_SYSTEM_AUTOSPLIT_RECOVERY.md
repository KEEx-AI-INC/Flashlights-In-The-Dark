## Assessment of converting the m.84 frame

Converting the m.84 frame would place m.77-83 and m.84-92 together on page 21.
The pair is likely to fit vertically because the first system becomes tacet at
m.81 and the second begins with five tacet measures. It is not the best
choice, however.

Its odd-page turns would include both:

- page 21, m.92 / m.93: unsafe for Shadow; Alto S and Baritone S tie from
  "hands:" into "together", while Light rests;
- page 23, m.98 / m.99: unsafe for Light; "hands grow / weary" continues and
  Alto L1/L2 ties.

The m.93 conversion retains the ideal m.83 turn and introduces only the latter
of those two compromises. It therefore strictly dominates the m.84 conversion
for page-turn safety while using an equally light, more directly proven pair.
